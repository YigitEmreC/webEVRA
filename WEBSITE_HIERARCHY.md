# Website Hierarchy

Use this document to outline the intended structure of the website.

## Site map

- Home
- Contact
- Information
- Products
- Packages
- Services
- Help and support


## Page details

### Home
- Details of the company
- Introduction
- A preview video of the setup

### [Contact]
- E-mail bot
- Standard contact information

### Information
- Overall information about the company and owners

### Products
- A full list of supported products
- Full prices of the listed products
- A feature to make a cart of wanted products

### Packages
- A variety of different packages consisting of different products suited for different contexts
- The packages should be orderable online, to be delivered later on (so a full checkout system that can input card information and do transactions)

### Services
- An explanation of the installation procedure
- An explanation of the support and maintenance after the installation

### Help and Support
- A portal to request maintenance or technical support
- A detailed F.A.Q. for easy troubleshooting
