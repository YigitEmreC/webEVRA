Troubleshooting

A device shows as "offline" or "unavailable" in the app
  1. Check that the device has power: plugged in, switched on at the wall, or fresh batteries.
  2. Check that your internet router and the smart home hub are both on.
  3. Wi-Fi devices: make sure your Wi-Fi name and password haven't changed recently.
  4. Battery sensors can take a few minutes to report back after a restart. Press the button on the sensor to wake it up.
  5. Still offline after 15 minutes? Send a support request and mention which device it is.
An automation didn't run (for example the lights didn't switch on)
  1. Check that all devices involved are online (see the question above).
  2. Check the time and conditions of the automation in the app, for example "only after sunset".
  3. If a motion sensor is involved: it only reports again after a short pause, so walk out of view for a minute and try again.

How do I replace the battery in a sensor?

Most sensors open by sliding or twisting off the back cover. Note the battery type printed inside (often a CR2032 or CR2450 coin cell), insert the new one the same way round and close the cover. The sensor reconnects by itself within a few minutes.
